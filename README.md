## 📖 About

🔹 **RimWorld — Character Editor** is a powerful tool built to help you customize every aspect of your colonists and pawns in RimWorld with an in-game editor. Edit appearance, backstory, traits, skills, health conditions, and relationships for any pawn without leaving your colony.

🔸 Whether you're a seasoned professional or just getting started, **RimWorld — Character Editor** delivers a clean, intuitive experience packed with everything you need — right out of the box. Join thousands of users who've already streamlined their RimWorld modding workflow. Download now and get started in minutes. 🚀

## 🚀 Features

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Full Stat Editing | Modify skills, passions, and aptitudes for any colonist |
| Appearance Customization | Change hair, body, skin tone, and equipment visually |
| Health Editor | Add, remove, or modify injuries, implants, and health conditions |
| Backstory Override | Assign custom backstories and childhood/adulthood backgrounds |
